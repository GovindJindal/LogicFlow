⚡ How to Wire

  1. Drag components to canvas
  2. Click a blue port (turns green)
  3. Click another port to connect
  4. Right-click any component or wire to delete
  5. Double-click component to edit values
  6. Long-press (0.6s) activates eraser mode
  7. Click ▶ Run to simulate circuit
