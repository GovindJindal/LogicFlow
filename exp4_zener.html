Key Concepts

🔵 Line regulation — Zener maintains Vout despite Vin changes.

🟡 Load regulation — Vout stays stable as RL varies.

🔴 Breakdown region — Unlike normal diodes, Zener operation requires reverse breakdown.

📐 Key Observations
BREAKDOWN VOLTAGE
Vz = 5.1V for 1N4733A. Output clamped above this Vin level.
REGULATION PRINCIPLE
Rs limits current. Excess current flows through Zener. RL sees stable Vz.
POWER LIMIT
Pz = Vz × Iz ≤ 500mW. Exceeding this destroys the Zener diode.
